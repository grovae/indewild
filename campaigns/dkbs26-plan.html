01The plan at a glance

  • One playbook, two stores. Same dates, same offers, same creative skeleton. Only the urgency channel differs: SMS supports the US, WhatsApp powers India.
  • 29% is the only number anyone ever sees. Clean, uncluttered, easy to say out loud.
  • A secret extra 5% lands in waitlist inboxes on Sep 4 — a reward for joining early, never advertised anywhere.
  • All of August builds the waitlist. Popup + ads + social from Aug 3. Promise: "the biggest sale of the year is coming." No price revealed.
  • Daily rhythm inside the sale. One message per day — a product spotlight or a 24-hour deal, never both. The base offer never changes underneath.
  • Subscribers are protected and included — their next order is auto-upgraded to the sale price, then we invite them to shop the rest of the shelf.
  • August stays quiet. No other promos before the sale, so 29% lands as the event of the year.
