Alia pop-up: the first weeks

Recommendation: keep Alia running through BFCM, review in mid-December. Signups are up roughly 3x in both stores after adjusting for store traffic. Revenue from new customers is up in India and still early in the US.

Store orders were flat in the US. In India they rose in October (Clarifying and Cherry Cola launches), so India is also shown per 100 store orders.

What this means

  • About 9,000 extra subscribers in India and 800 in the US in the first weeks
  • The US SMS list is growing for the first time, about 300 a week
  • All of them are on the list before BFCM, the biggest sale of the quarter
  • India is already seeing more first orders. The US has two weeks of data so far

Watch-outs

  • Each signup converts less often: India 12% → 6%, US 18% → 7% within 7 days. More people sign up, fewer are ready to buy straight away
  • More orders use the 10% welcome code: India 11% → 20%, US 7% → 13%. Some of these would have bought at full price
  • The welcome series needs to work harder on these new subscribers

How this was measured

Source: Klaviyo, both stores. Before = the weeks just before Alia went live, with the Klaviyo pop-up running and the birthday sale excluded.

Signups = pop-up email signups, including Alia signups that reached Klaviyo through Shopify. Checkout opt-ins are excluded.

First orders = a new subscriber's first order within 7 days of signing up. Signups from the last 7 days are excluded, as they haven't had time to convert yet.

Traffic-adjusted = first orders per 100 store orders, before vs after, applied to October's order volume.
