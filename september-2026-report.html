What September taught us that changes the plan
Sign-ups pause during a sale0 new subscriptions, 30 Aug – 7 Sep

Subscription checkouts stopped completely while the 29% sale price was live. Before BFCM we'll confirm the subscribe option stays visible and is priced at or below the sale price, so sale shoppers can still start one.

Too much product, not the wrong product13 of 42 cancellations

Most of the "more than I need" cancellations came right after a renewal. We'll add a "push my next order back" option to the cancel flow for this reason, so a subscriber who has too much product can delay their next order instead of cancelling.

Waitlists make small lists pay298 people · ₹19.5K on launch day

The Clarifying Shampoo waitlist repeated what the Birthday Sale waitlist showed: a small group who asked to hear first buys on the first day. The US shampoo launch and BFCM both open with a waitlist.

US non-sale sends go to buyers3 sends · $1,426 · 520 unsubscribes

After the sale, the survey, Champi Kit and Gift Shop sends went to lists of 23,000 to 57,000. In October, US sends outside the launch and the promo go to buyer segments only.
