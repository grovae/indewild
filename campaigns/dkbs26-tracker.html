Method · refreshed 3 September 2026 · 17:56 IST / 08:26 EDT

Where the numbers come from. Sale revenue and orders are Klaviyo’s Placed Order metric, bucketed to local calendar days — IST for India, Eastern for the US. India 2024 and 2025 reconcile to the Shopify total sales export within 0.6%. CRM revenue is Klaviyo campaign and flow attributed revenue across each sale window.

Sale windows. India ran 28–30 August in 2024, 28–31 August in 2025, and 28 August to 5 September this year. The US ran 28–31 August in 2024, 28 August to 2 September in 2025 with the Labor Day extension, and 28 August to 5 September this year.

Day one across years. In 2024 both markets ran a preview on the 28th and in 2025 both opened with early access that day. This year the 28th was warm-up only, so 2026 begins selling on the 29th. Every year is anchored to 28 August so the days line up.

Today is still running. The most recent bar in each chart, and every “to date” figure, covers a day that has not finished. Both will rise before the next refresh.

WhatsApp, India only. India’s WhatsApp figures come from Bitespeed and cover campaigns scheduled inside the sale window, plus the birthday-sale automations that went live during it. Evergreen automations are excluded because the export reports their revenue since each flow went live rather than by date, so the WhatsApp total is a floor. 2024 and 2025 had no WhatsApp programme — Bitespeed’s earliest broadcast is 27 February 2026 — so those years are email-only as a matter of fact, and the year-on-year table stays on email to keep the comparison honest. The US store has no WhatsApp.
