DKBS'26 runs 29 August to 5 September across both stores. Plans are with the team, copy and design are in production, and WhatsApp templates go to Bitespeed for approval ahead of each phase. Base discount is 29%, with 34% held for the waitlist. US subscribers get a one-cycle renewal match.

What July taught us that changes the plan
Offers beat education₹3.23L from two sends

The launch and the free-product offer out-earned every explainer send in both stores. DKBS is built around a different concrete offer each day rather than a run-up of product education.

Two channels, one momentRoughly 2× the return

Ice Ice Baby and the B1G1 each ran on email and WhatsApp together and earned about double what either channel managed alone. Every DKBS deal day will run both.

Full-list sends are expensive2,116 US unsubscribes

Four US sends to roughly 52,000 people each cost between 264 and 384 unsubscribes and returned under $530 apiece. For DKBS we're reserving full-base sends for two days only and segmenting the rest, so the list is in good shape going into the sale.

SMS behaves differently by market$1,330 US · ₹0.18 per ₹1 India

US SMS came back on after two months and earned $1,330 from four sends, more per person messaged than email. India's one SMS blast reached 155,928 handsets and returned ₹5,543 against ₹31,186 of send cost. US SMS stays in the DKBS plan; India stays on WhatsApp, where every rupee spent came back as ₹2.28.
