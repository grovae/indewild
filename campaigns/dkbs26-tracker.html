Method · refreshed 7 September 2026 · 13:02 IST / 03:32 EDT

What counts as retention revenue. Klaviyo campaign and flow attributed revenue, plus Bitespeed WhatsApp campaign and automation revenue for India. Share of sale is that figure over Placed Order revenue for the same window, bucketed to local calendar days — IST for India, Eastern for the US.

Sale windows. 2024 ran 28–31 August and 2025 ran 28 August to 1 September, both confirmed by the indē wild team. 2026 runs 28 August to 5 September and is still open, so its figures are still building.

WhatsApp, India only. Campaigns scheduled inside the window, plus the birthday-sale automations that went live during it. Evergreen automations are excluded because the export reports revenue since each flow went live rather than by date, so the WhatsApp total is a floor. 2024 and 2025 had no WhatsApp programme, so the year-on-year comparison is email-only and shown as the darker bar segment.

Status. Final figures. The sale closed on 5 September and the window is complete. Store revenue and orders appear only as the denominator for retention share. WhatsApp automation revenue is carried from the 3 September journeys export, which has not been re-issued, so India's retention total is a floor.
