01

September in brief

  1. India's Birthday Sale campaigns more than doubled last year's.

    Over the full sale, 29 August to 5 September, India's email sends made ₹33.4L against ₹15.8L in 2025, with WhatsApp adding ₹26.3L on top. US sends made $23.9K, in line with last year's $24.9K.

  2. India's automated emails nearly doubled.

    Flows brought in ₹15.2L in September, up from ₹8.4L. The Birthday Sale welcome and cart flows made ₹12.0L of it, and the everyday flows kept running underneath.

  3. US subscription renewals were up 35%. New sign-ups paused during the sale.

    100 renewal orders shipped, up from 74. No new subscriptions started for nine days while the sale price was live, so the active base held at 602. We're fixing this before BFCM.

07

What September changes for October

Keep subscriptions open in a sale0 sign-ups, 30 Aug – 7 Sep

Before BFCM we'll make sure the subscribe option stays visible and is priced at or below the sale price, so sale shoppers can still start a subscription.

Offer a delay, not just cancel13 of 42 cancellations

Add a "push my next order back" option to the cancel flow for "more than I need". Champi subscribers also get a refill reminder timed to their 90-day cycle in mid-October.

Open with a waitlist298 people · ₹19.5K on launch day

The Clarifying Shampoo waitlist group bought on day one, just like the Birthday Sale waitlist did. The US shampoo launch (8 Oct) and BFCM (waitlist from 28 Oct) both open this way.

US everyday sends to buyers3 sends · $1,426 · 520 unsubscribes

After the sale, three US sends went to lists of 23,000 to 57,000 people. In October, US sends outside the launch and the mid-month promo go to buyer segments only.

Grovae · indē wild · September 2026 · how these numbers are measured
Revenue from retention = Klaviyo email, SMS and flow revenue (attributed on Placed Order, Klaviyo default attribution) plus Bitespeed WhatsApp and SMS broadcast revenue. Klaviyo and Bitespeed attribute independently, so an order that came from both an email and a WhatsApp message can show up in both. Sale = sends and automations built for the Birthday Sale, 29 Aug – 7 Sep (US Last Chance went out on 7 Sep). Everyday = everything else, including the thank-you and survey sends. WhatsApp counts main broadcast rows only (retries are not counted twice), and send cost is the cost Bitespeed reports. WhatsApp automated journeys are not included, because Bitespeed reports them as all-time totals that can't be split by month, so India's figure is understated by that amount. Subscriptions come from Recharge: new and cancelled are counted by start and cancel date, active is rebuilt at month end, and renewals are Recharge recurring orders. 2025 sale figures are Klaviyo campaigns over 28 Aug – 2 Sep 2025.
